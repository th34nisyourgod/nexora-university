NX‑ABOUT

A campus built like a network, not a hierarchy.

Most universities organize knowledge into silos. Nexora organizes it into a graph. Every school sits one edge away from every other — an engineer can co-author with a sculptor, a physician can sit in on a philosophy seminar, and a first-year can walk into a lab that's publishing in Nature. We call the connective tissue between disciplines "the nexus," and it shows up everywhere: in our shared studios, our cross-listed courses, and the way our transcripts read less like a checklist and more like a map.
